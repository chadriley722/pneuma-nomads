we didn't know florida had a greek town until we were standing in one.

tarpon springs sits on the gulf just north of tampa. the main strip is dodecanese boulevard, right along the sponge docks, with boats on one side and little shops on the other.

## hellas

first stop was hellas restaurant & bakery. greek food and pastries in a greek town. it made sense.

## wandering

after that we just walked. the docks, the side streets, the shops. it feels like a little piece of somewhere else dropped onto the florida coast.

then we drove around a bit before heading back to camp.
