started the morning with what may or may not have been a scorpion in my coffee. great omen.

## the leak

we've known something was off in the pass-through storage for a while. Lucy — our cat — kept trying to squeeze herself back there, which is what actually tipped us off. Daniel was worried she'd pee back there (valid concern), so we pulled the wall and went in to check.

and there it was. a slow drip from a blue lever fitting on the freshwater line. not dramatic. just a quiet little drip that, left alone, turns into a big expensive problem.

i tightened the fitting a quarter turn. we argued briefly about righty tighty lefty loosey. Daniel won. then we waited.

## smoothie time

while the fitting sat there being either fixed or not fixed, i made us smoothies.

we've been intermittent fasting lately — eating between 12 and 6 — and trying to eat healthier and cheaper on the road. the smoothie has become a daily thing. base is always two bananas, peanut butter, overnight oats, flax seed, frozen fruit. today i added cranberry juice because we had it. 100% not from concentrate. strong stuff. i eyeball everything. it tastes a little different every day.

## the verdict

went back to check. no drip. nothing on the floor.

fixed. probably.
