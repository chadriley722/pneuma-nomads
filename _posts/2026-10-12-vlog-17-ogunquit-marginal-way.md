no driving today. we walked from camp into ogunquit the long way.

## the walk in

through the marsh, down the beach and into the village for coffee.

## the marginal way

then the marginal way, the path that runs along the rocks right above the ocean. we walked it out past the little lighthouse and made a new friend along the way.

## lunch

lunch at bandito's mexican grill.

## the walk home

back down ogunquit beach and along the marsh road home. we spotted a heron on the way. a slow, easy day on the coast.
