we'd been stuck in the rv for a couple days. florida rain. the kind that just doesn't stop.

when it finally let up, i noticed the wildlife preserve that backs right up to the campground. Upper Hillsboro Wildlife Management Area. free primitive camping at the entrance, managed by the Southwest Florida Water Management District. public land. no excuse not to go.

## the hike in

there's no trail access directly from the rv park — you have to go out to the main road and back in through the actual entrance. worth the extra walk.

on the way in i saw a tortoise, a snake that had clearly seen better days, some mushrooms, and what i was told was a gut drop. don't ask.

the two guys at the entrance shack waved me in. then mentioned, almost as an afterthought, that there was a turkey hunt happening inside. just don't make any turkey noises and you'll be fine.

great.

## on trees

at some point i stopped and looked at a specific tree and thought. wow, that is a beautiful tree. then i thought about why that tree and not any of the others around it. couldn't figure it out.

if you're a tree reading this: the universe made you exactly right. don't worry about it.

## hammock time

found a spot near the road on the quieter side of the preserve. set up the hammock. relaxed for about two minutes. got absolutely destroyed by a bug. retreated into the closed cocoon version of the hammock and stayed there.

came out feeling like a new person. ten minutes in nature and everything just resets. the anxiety, the noise, the spiral — all of it goes quiet. the world is always okay. it's just my mind that forgets sometimes.

## on the way home

met a tortoise. we had a moment. he knew.

made it back just as the rain started again.
