we drove down from maine to salem in the middle of october. which means half of new england had the same idea.

## downtown

we wandered downtown and through salem common. costumes, witch hats and a lot of people.

## derby wharf

we grabbed a coffee at odd meter and walked derby wharf all the way out to the little lighthouse. the calmest part of the day.

## back into the crowds

then back through the crowds and artists' row.

## the witch house and the garden

we found some quiet at the witch house and the ropes mansion garden, then took chestnut street on the way out. if you go in october, go early.
